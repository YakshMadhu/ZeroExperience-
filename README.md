# ZeroExperience-
This platform introduces people with zero real word experience to real life projects. It doesn't require resume, approval, or assessment. The focus is on the result instead of  qualifications. To maintain objectivity, first approved solution accepted.

# Personal problem: What am I facing?

<img width="596" height="612" alt="image" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/44f97bc7-f45e-4bdf-a022-a6885e4d576a" />

[Description: **Ouroboros** represents a cycle that keeps feeding itself. Reflects the current entry-level job market]

* Junior entering a difficult job market.
* Entry-level roles with huge applicant pools.
* Huge applicant pools lead to intense competition.
* Intense competition makes everyone try to out-qualify everyone else.
* Entry barrier keeps rising.
* Entry-level roles start requiring prior experience.
* Employers face too many similar candidates.
* Choose experienced candidates to reduce training costs and risk.
* With no experience, harder to get an opportunity.
* With no opportunity, harder to get experience.
* The process becomes slow, stressful, competitive, uncertain, and confusing.
* Pushes students toward endless applications, internships, personal projects, networking, and interviews.

# Goal: What do I want to achieve?

<img width="2044" height="1193" alt="image" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/cb7a869f-c400-4f76-9fb5-3d4fb4ffebac" />

* Equal opportunity, visibility, accessibility, and treatment for all participants regardless of their experience.
* No resume, past experiences, projects, approval, or education required.
* Users focus on participation, not competition
* Judge solutions, not people.
* Lower barriers to real-world experience.
* Prevent subjective selection by task posters.
* Avoid generic jobs like data annotation, translation, surveys, questionnaires, data collection.
* Prioratize, jobs are highly resistant to AI automation, and requires problem discovery, research, investigation, relationships, and decision.
